Preprints and work in progress

  • Goodness-of-fit for distributions on metric spaces

    Serrano, D., García-Portugués, E. and Van Keilegom, I. Preprint, forthcoming.

    A general goodness-of-fit framework for distributions on separable metric spaces, built on distance profiles. It covers simple and composite null hypotheses, incorporating the estimation of unknown parameters through a Bahadur-type expansion, and establishes the asymptotic null distribution of the test statistics, their consistency against fixed alternatives, and their weak limits under local alternatives.

  • Out-of-bag prediction balls for random forests in metric spaces

    Serrano, D. and García-Portugués, E. (2025). arXiv:2510.04299.

    Confidence regions around predictions from any bagged regression algorithm with metric-space-valued responses, built from the out-of-bag observations generated in a single forest training. Asymptotic guarantees for four coverage types, and smaller, more stable balls than split-conformal methods on the sphere, the hyperboloid and the space of positive definite matrices. Applied to solar dynamics.
